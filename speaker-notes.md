# Speaker notes (about 10 minutes)

A corrected script based on the recorded talk. Use it to re-record the video or to present live. Changes from the recording are marked **(fixed)**.

*A worked example. The company and specific markets have been anonymised; operational metrics are illustrative. See the note at the top of [business-case.md](business-case.md).*

**Opening (0:00).** I'm Ajay, from a European commercial team at a multinational commerce company. I have been with the company for over a year. What stands out is how much technology and supply-chain knowledge we combine, and the people I work with, including colleagues from the Asian headquarters who come to Europe to support our expansion.

**The idea (1:00).** I'm proposing that we use AI to turn the knowledge each of us holds into knowledge the whole team can use, so the company can grow across Europe.

**The problem (1:40).** We work across many tools and have formal and informal conversations with clients. A lot of what we learn stays in our heads. I write things in the CRM, but call notes, LinkedIn and email are not connected.
- Preparing for a client call takes **90–120 minutes (fixed: the recording said "more than 30 minutes")**.
- Follow-up completion is only 37%, against a 60% benchmark.
- A new rep takes **six to nine months to become productive (fixed: the recording said "six months to close a deal")**. Our average deal cycle is 42 days against a 35-day benchmark.

**What we'd do (3:00).** Combine the data we already have: call transcripts, email threads, notes and proposals. Reps go into calls and follow-ups with context, save time, have better conversations about client pain points, and move deals faster.

**Why now (4:00).** We have been in Europe for almost three years. Headquarters needs to see what we are learning, and today we do not track it. Because there is no system, the same few people get asked every time. They burn out and leave, and that limits our expansion.

**Three priorities (5:00).** Operational excellence (people feel empowered and see their contribution), knowledge for new hires in new countries, and growth in Europe. We use what our teams in Asia and Europe know to train new hires.

**How it works (5:50).** The AI needs context and constant input from us. Every conversation makes it smarter, and our feedback trains it. It learns the cultural nuances of each country in Europe, which competitors cannot easily copy. Reps benefit by winning more deals and by understanding what blocked deals before.

**Cost of doing nothing (6:50).** Inefficiency costs about **€180,000 a year (fixed: the recording said "180,000 years")**, for 7 senior reps alone. It also holds back expansion, because we do not know where we win and lose.

**Investment (7:20).** These are estimates. AI is not plug-and-play; it is iterative. Three-year cost is **€364,000** and the base case pays back in about **14 months**. Net benefit is about €870,000.

**Phases and gates (8:15).** Phase 0 is a four-week readiness check: relevant data, co-design with reps, legal and GDPR sign-off, and a security design sign-off. Only if the gates are met do we move to a 13-week pilot with 5–7 reps. The most we can lose is €109,000. We review at Day 45 and decide at Day 90.

**Projection (8:45).** The base case assumes **75% of the modelled benefit** arrives **(fixed: not "75% adoption")**. NPV is about €626,000. If only half arrives it is about €313,000. If only prep-time savings count it is about €268,000.

**Governance, adoption, risk and trust (9:15).** There are named owners, including a new AI Owner role. We track adoption and data quality from day one. Risks are scored and mitigated. The human is always in control: the AI suggests, reps decide, and everything is labelled as a suggestion.

**Close (10:15).** In 90 days we would have real evidence from our own data, clients and people. Today I'm asking for one thing: approve a short review and initial assessment of this proposal, with no spend. Legal, IT security, Sales Ops and HR would look at it over about two weeks and give a go or no-go on the Phase 0 readiness check (€15,000). Phase 1 (€94,000) would follow only if the Phase 0 gates are met. Thank you. I'm happy to go deeper in Q&A.
