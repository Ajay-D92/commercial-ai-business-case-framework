## Three answers to practise out loud
1. "What is the most I can lose, and when do I find out?"
2. "What would make you stop?" (use the stop number from the baseline template)
3. "Why not buy an off-the-shelf tool?" It costs less on paper. We justify the custom build by our own cross-cultural knowledge and our control of the data.
